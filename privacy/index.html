Privacy Policy

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 10 日 · Last updated: September 10, 2026

Blurly（“本 App”）是一款在设备本地处理照片的隐私保护工具。我们重视你的隐私，并以不收集数据为产品设计原则。

1. 我们不收集任何数据

本 App 不要求注册账号，不包含广告、分析或第三方追踪 SDK，也不会将你的照片、识别结果或使用信息发送给开发者或任何第三方。本 App 不发起网络请求，不存在开发者服务器或云端处理。

2. 照片与识别处理

你选择的照片仅在设备上通过 Apple Vision、Core Image 等系统框架完成识别和打码。照片不会上传到服务器。系统照片选择器由 iOS 提供，本 App 不会扫描或读取你的完整相册。

3. 相册权限

只有在你主动选择保存处理结果时，本 App 才会申请“添加照片到相册”权限。该权限仅用于写入你生成的图片。本 App 不访问相机、通讯录、麦克风或定位。

4. 人脸数据处理 · Face Data

本 App 会对你主动选择处理的照片执行人脸区域检测，用于生成打码遮罩。具体如下：

  • 收集什么：仅从你选择的照片中计算人脸在画面中的位置坐标（矩形区域）。本 App 不提取、不存储、不生成任何人脸特征值或生物识别标识，不建立人脸库，不进行人脸识别或身份比对。人脸数据仅来自你在系统照片选择器中主动选中的那一张照片；本 App 无法访问相机。
  • 用途：唯一用途是在当前编辑会话中对该区域施加模糊、马赛克或纯色遮罩，并导出你确认后的结果图。不用于广告、画像或任何其他目的。
  • 存储位置：仅存在于你设备的内存与临时缓存中（Apple Vision 在设备端运行）。不存在开发者服务器，也不存在于任何云端。
  • 是否共享：不共享。本 App 不向任何第三方（包括任何第三方 AI 服务、广告平台或分析服务）传输人脸数据、照片内容或任何衍生数据。不存在数据出境传输。
  • 保留期限：不保留。人脸区域数据仅在本次编辑会话期间存在，会话结束或关闭 App 时即随内存释放删除；开发者侧没有任何留存副本。
  • 如何删除：退出编辑或关闭 App 即自动清除。你保存到相册的结果图由你自行管理并可随时删除。
  • 是否联网：本 App 不发起网络请求，人脸数据处理全程离线完成。

5. 第三方服务与第三方 AI

本 App 通过 Apple App Store 分发。Apple 可能依据其自身隐私政策处理与下载、购买、设备相关的信息。

本 App 不使用任何第三方 AI 服务。人脸检测、文字识别与打码全部由 Apple 系统框架在设备本地完成，不向任何第三方传输数据。就本政策所述功能版本，本 App 不嵌入任何第三方数据收集、分析或广告 SDK。

6. 数据使用与共享

我们不会将你的数据用于追踪、广告定向或出售；不会与第三方共享你的照片内容；也不会将其用于本政策未说明的任何用途。

7. 儿童隐私

由于本 App 不收集任何用户数据，因此不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 数据安全与保留

因为在设备之外不存在数据传输与存储，故不涉及服务器端的数据安全与保留问题。处理过程中的图像数据保留在设备内存与临时缓存中，仅用于生成本次编辑的预览与导出。

9. 政策变更

如果未来功能变化导致数据处理方式发生改变，我们会更新本页面，并在适用情况下于 App 内说明。

10. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请前往 Blurly 支持中心提交问题。

English

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Blurly (“the App”) is a photo privacy tool that processes everything on your device. We respect your privacy, and not collecting data is a product design principle.

1. We Do Not Collect Any Data

The App requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDK. It does not send your photos, recognition results, or usage information to the developer or to any third party. The App makes no network requests and has no developer server or cloud processing.

2. Photos and On-Device Recognition

Photos you select are processed on the device only, using Apple system frameworks such as Vision and Core Image. Photos are never uploaded. The system photo picker is provided by iOS, and the App does not scan or read your full photo library.

3. Photo Library Permission

The App requests “Add Photos” permission only when you choose to save a result. It is used solely to write the image you generated. The App does not access the camera, contacts, microphone, or location.

4. Face Data

The App performs face region detection on photos you explicitly select, in order to place a masking overlay. Specifically:

  • What is collected: only the positional coordinates (bounding box) of a face within the photo you selected. The App does not extract, store, or create any face template, faceprint, or biometric identifier; it does not build a face database; it does not perform face recognition or identity matching. Face data originates solely from the single photo you actively select in the system photo picker; the App has no camera access.
  • Use: solely to apply a blur, mosaic, or solid-fill mask over that region in the current editing session and to export the user-confirmed result image. Not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.
  • Storage location: in the memory and temporary cache of your own device only (Apple Vision runs on-device). There is no developer server and no cloud storage.
  • Sharing: Not shared. The App transmits no face data, photo content, or derived data to any third party, including any third-party AI service, advertising platform, or analytics service. There is no cross-border transfer, because no data leaves the device.
  • Retention: Not retained. Face region data exists only for the duration of the editing session and is released when the session ends or the App is closed. No copy is retained on the developer side.
  • Deletion: cleared automatically when you exit the editor or close the App. Result images you save to your photo library are under your control and can be deleted at any time.
  • Network: the App makes no network requests; all face data processing is fully offline.

5. Third-Party Services and Third-Party AI

The App is distributed via the Apple App Store. Apple may process download-, purchase-, and device-related information under its own privacy policy.

The App does not use any third-party AI service. Face detection, text recognition, and masking are performed entirely on-device by Apple system frameworks, and no data is transmitted to any third party. For the feature set described in this policy, the App embeds no third-party data-collection, analytics, or advertising SDK.

6. Data Use and Sharing

We do not use your data for tracking or ad targeting, do not sell it, do not share photo contents with third parties, and do not use it for any purpose not described in this policy.

7. Children’s Privacy

Because the App collects no user data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security and Retention

Because no data is transmitted or stored off the device, there is no server-side security or retention to address. Image data during processing stays in device memory and temporary cache, used only to produce the preview and export for the current edit.

9. Changes

If future feature changes alter how data is handled, we will update this page and, where applicable, explain within the App.

10. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at the Blurly support center.
